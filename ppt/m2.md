# Siddhantic Astronomy

- Based on texts called Siddhantas, meaning established doctrines or proven theories
- Written between 500 CE and 1400 CE by various astronomers
- Used mathematics such as trigonometry and algebra to calculate the positions and motions of planets and stars
- Explained eclipses, equinoxes, solstices and other phenomena

![bg right](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4f/Aryabhata.jpg/800px-Aryabhata.jpg)

# Colonial and Modern Astronomy

- Indian astronomy was rediscovered by European scholars in the 18th century
- India became a partner in some of the mega projects of astronomy in the 20th and 21st centuries
- Examples: Giant Metrewave Radio Telescope, Indian Astronomical Observatory, AstroSat, Chandrayaan, Aditya
- India also has a vibrant community of amateur astronomers and enthusiasts
