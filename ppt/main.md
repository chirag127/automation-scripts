# Introduction

- Astronomy has a rich history in India
- Ancient Indian astronomers made significant contributions to the field
- Key texts: Vedas, Siddhantas, and Aryabhatiya

![bg right](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/37/Sanskrit_Manuscript_Wellcome_L0070805.jpg)

# Pre-Vedic and Vedic Astronomy

- Some of the earliest forms of astronomy can be dated to the Indus Valley civilization or earlier
- Some cosmological concepts are present in the Vedas, such as the movement of heavenly bodies and the course of the year
- The Rig Veda describes time as a wheel with 12 parts and 360 spokes (days), with a remainder of 5, making reference to the solar calendar
- Vedanga Jyotisha: the science of lights or celestial bodies
- The Vedanga Jyotisha is the oldest known text on astronomy, dating to 1400–1200 BCE
- Lunisolar calendar: combines lunar phases and solar seasons
- Nakshatras: 27 or 28 segments of the sky corresponding to stars or constellations

---

# Siddhantas Astronomy

- Based on texts called Siddhantas, meaning established doctrines or proven theories
- Written between 500 CE and 1400 CE by various astronomers
- Used mathematics such as trigonometry and algebra to calculate the positions and motions of planets and stars
- Explained eclipses, equinoxes, solstices and other phenomena

![bg right](https://www.sakya.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/buddhism-classes-canberra.jpg)

# Aryabhata

- Born in 476 CE
- Aryabhatiya: Major work in astronomy and mathematics
- Heliocentric model, accurate calculation of Earth's circumference.
- Motions of the solar system, planetary orbits, and eclipses.
- Sidereal periods


![bg left](https://www.indianetzone.com/photos_gallery/39/Aryabhata_24762.jpg)

# Other Siddhantas

## Varahamihira

- Born in 505 CE
- Wrote Brihat Samhita and Panchasiddhantika
- Contributions to astrology, mathematics, and calendar systems

## Bhaskaracharya

- Born in 1114 CE
- Wrote Siddhanta Shiromani
- Calculated Earth's circumference, gravity, and more

# Conclusion

- Ancient Indian astronomers made significant contributions to astronomy
- Their work laid the foundation for future discoveries
- A rich legacy that continues to inspire modern science
